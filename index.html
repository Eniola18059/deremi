On turning twenty-two

Some birthdays are just a number. This one arrives with a degree, a professional title and a year that has already changed what your name stands for.

You finished school as a Registered Nurse. That is years of long shifts, exams and steadiness under pressure, and you did it with grace.

Today is for you: rest, be celebrated, and let the people around you do the carrying for once.

What people notice about you

Care

You show up for people when it counts, and you make them feel like the only person in the room.

Voice

I love the way you speak. It is soft, bright and gentle, and even ordinary words sound like a story when you say them. I could listen to you all day.

Poise

Calm is a skill, and you carry it well. People settle when you walk in.

Depth

You take your work, your faith and your people seriously, and you still know how to laugh.

Drive

You finish what you start. Twenty-two looks like the beginning of a long, well-earned run.

For the year ahead

May every door you knock on open early.

May your hands heal, and your own heart stay whole.

May favour find you in rooms you have not yet entered.

May your rest be as deep as your work is good.

May you grow in wisdom faster than you grow in years.

May joy be an ordinary thing in your days.

Answer honestly.
